Purchases and Full Access

Monthly and lifetime Full Access are purchased through Apple. Monthly access renews until canceled; lifetime is a one-time purchase. Both include the current paid tools, including Course Weather in the updated app. Prices and purchase terms appear before Apple checkout.

To recover access, open More → Settings → Full access → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. If the purchase is pending or has expired or been refunded, access will reflect Apple's purchase status. Contact us if a valid purchase is still missing.

Manage or cancel an Apple subscription · Request an Apple purchase refund

Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel a monthly subscription. You can manage monthly renewal through your Apple Account.

Protect your Library and golf records

Keep the app installed when troubleshooting so its local Library is preserved. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step.

More → Settings → Backup & Restore can export golf data and notes or manage optional iCloud backups. Backup and restore are free. Compact golf-data backups do not include original videos, photos or audio. Keep important recordings separately in Photos or Files.

Library storage and recovery controls can help with recently deleted Range sessions or preserved recordings. Avoid permanently deleting media you still need.

Recording and tracing

For best results, use ample light, keep the camera steady and keep a clear view of the golf ball. Set Ball Lock on the ball before tracing. Predicted flight and landing are visual estimates, and results depend on the footage.

Check camera, microphone, Photos and location permissions in iPhone Settings if a related feature is unavailable. Unrelated features remain available when a permission is declined.

Course Weather

With verified monthly or lifetime Full Access, tap the weather bar on the Play map for current conditions, the updated time, Apple Weather attribution and data-source links. Weather requires an internet connection and an available location. Refreshing too frequently will not improve the weather provider's measurement cadence.

Privacy and your choices

Read the PURE ARC Privacy Policy for details about local recordings, optional backups, maps, weather, purchases and deletion. Email the support address above with a privacy request. There is no separate PURE ARC account to sign into or delete.
