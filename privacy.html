PURE ARC is provided by Jordan Weber ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use PURE ARC, including golf recording, video editing, Ghost comparison, course information, Rules assistance, purchases, and optional golf-data backup features.

For privacy questions or requests, contact purearc@proton.me.

Your recordings and golf information

PURE ARC stores its working Library on your device. Depending on the features you use, this includes:

  • Recorded or imported swing videos and their audio; imported sounds and recorded voice-overs; thumbnails; and edited or exported video files.
  • Tracers, drawings, video trims, audio placements, Ghost reference selections, comparison names, favorites, shot labels, and notes.
  • Golf shot results and estimates, club information, scores, saved courses, rounds, Journal records, and saved Rules conversations.
  • Capture details such as recording time, video format, and camera information. Capture can retain device-motion measurements when the relevant recording tools are enabled. If you allow location access, shots and rounds can also contain coordinates, accuracy information, direction, altitude, and pin or ball positions.
  • App preferences and paid-feature defaults. Historical installations may retain earlier local usage counters; basic two-swing Ghost comparisons are free and unlimited.

Video tracing, impact and swing analysis, Ghost body/timing estimates, audio editing, and scorecard text recognition run on your device. Body measurements are used to compare golf swings; the app does not use them to identify a person. The app does not send your recordings, scorecard images, or Rules questions to an external AI service for analysis. Optional golf-data backups, described below, can send selected structured records and notes to your private iCloud storage. PURE ARC does not require a separate PURE ARC online account.

Ask PURE uses reviewed rules saved in the app, with Apple's on-device language model when it is supported and available. Typed questions, dictated text, and answers can be saved in your local Rules history. Dictation starts when you tap the microphone and uses available on-device speech recognition; the app does not fall back to server speech recognition. The dictation feature does not save an additional voice-recording file. Saved Rules conversations can be included in a golf-data backup if you choose to export or enable iCloud backup.

Where personal ball-learning tools are enabled, the app may keep local ball appearance examples, source-recording references, and personal analysis profiles. These support checks on future tracing on the device. The app does not upload them for external model training, and these learning/model files are excluded from compact golf-data backups. Available personal-learning controls let you disable learning or reset its examples and profiles without deleting videos or saved traces.

Your device or file provider may store or synchronize copies through services you have enabled, such as device backups, iCloud Photos, or a cloud storage provider selected through Files. Those services operate under their own settings and policies and are separate from the optional golf-data backups described next.

Free golf-data backup and restoration

Backup and restore are standard free features. A subscription or lifetime purchase is not required.

Includes golf data and notes. Videos and photos are not included.

A compact backup can include Journal entries, round dates and notes, hole scores and pars, saved course and tee details, handicap-related records, My Bag club profiles and distances, practice/shot measurements, Rules conversations, comparison summaries, saved reference selections, and supported preferences. It may contain locations saved with your golf records. Stable record identifiers help keep related records connected after restoration.

Compact backups exclude video, photos, audio files, thumbnails, downloaded maps, caches, AI models and personal-learning files, credentials, purchase entitlements, and device-specific media paths. Measurements and notes can be restored without the original recording. Media must be kept separately if you want to replay it on another device.

Export Backup creates a portable file and opens Files so you choose where to save it, including local storage, iCloud Drive or another available file provider. A file kept only inside the app's storage may disappear when the app is deleted. A local-only backup must be transferred to another device before that device can restore it. Portable backup files are compressed and checked for integrity, but are not password-protected by PURE ARC. Choose their storage and sharing locations accordingly.

If you enable Automatic iCloud Backup, PURE ARC saves compressed golf-data backups in the private CloudKit database associated with your system iCloud account. This uses your available iCloud storage. Automatic backup is off by default. Back Up Now can request a one-off backup without enabling ongoing backups. Local changes are saved first; uploads wait for connectivity and the execution time iOS makes available. The app does not promise uninterrupted background uploading or instant synchronization between devices.

The backup service keeps up to ten completed recovery versions. In addition to the backup itself, iCloud stores its identifier, date, size, record count, format version and integrity-check information. The app keeps account-scoped queue and retry information locally so it can safely resume pending work and avoid mixing iCloud accounts. Private CloudKit content belongs to your iCloud account and is not made publicly visible or placed in a PURE ARC-operated database. Apple provides CloudKit and handles its service under Apple's privacy practices.

Import and iCloud restoration show a preview and require confirmation before changing local golf data. Conflicting edits require a choice. Restoration keeps existing local media and creates a local recovery snapshot. It does not restore or grant purchases; use Restore purchases for paid access.

Turning off automatic backup stops future automatic uploads but does not delete previous iCloud versions. Deleting a record locally can leave it in an earlier recovery version until that version is replaced or cloud data is removed. Manage previous cloud data through Apple's iCloud storage controls. Deleting PURE ARC from your phone does not necessarily delete exported files or existing iCloud backups. If you change iCloud accounts, review that account's backups and enable automatic backup again if desired.

Permissions and your choices

PURE ARC requests access when needed for the relevant feature:

  • Camera: to record golf swings or scan a scorecard.
  • Microphone: to capture swing audio, help locate ball contact, or record a voice-over or dictate an Ask PURE question when you start that action.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe an Ask PURE question on your device when the available speech-recognition path requires this permission.
  • Photos and Files: to import media you select, and to save exported videos to Photos when you choose that action.
  • Location: to support course maps, live GPS yardages, shot and pin marking, Birdseye maps, and local weather. The app requests location while in use; it does not request always-on background location access.
  • Motion: to support capture tools that measure movement of the recording device when enabled. These measurements stay with local capture information.
  • Notifications: to schedule optional Daily PURE reminders on your device. The current app does not use a PURE ARC push-notification server.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Declining a permission can make the related feature unavailable without preventing use of unrelated features. Turning off permission does not automatically erase information already saved.

Maps, course information, and weather

These optional parts of the app use network services, even though video editing and the Library operate locally:

  • Apple Maps: course searches and map displays use Apple services. Search text and the area being displayed may be sent to Apple. Map areas can be based on your location, a saved shot, or a course you select.
  • Apple Weather: Course Weather requires an active monthly or lifetime Full Access purchase. When access is verified, weather requests send location coordinates rounded to three decimal places to Apple WeatherKit to obtain local conditions. Rounding reduces detail but does not make the location anonymous.
  • OpenGolf API (api.opengolfapi.org): course search sends the text you enter. Loading course information, map features, or conditions sends the selected course identifier and, where relevant, hole number. The plays-like-distance feature sends distance in yards and, when available, wind speed and direction, shot bearing, temperature, altitude, and elevation difference. Its current request does not contain your video, audio, notes, or raw latitude/longitude. OpenGolf's published privacy policy describes request metadata including IP address, browser/client information, requested path and time, with server logs retained for 30 days. It uses IP addresses to enforce service limits and identifies hosting providers that handle request metadata. PURE ARC does not use its account signup, contribution, or public edit-submission features.
  • Optional detailed course provider (api.golfcourseapi.com): if this integration is configured, course searches and detail requests send the search text or selected course identifier and the configured provider API key. Its privacy policy describes API usage records, including request counts and timestamps.

Online services also receive connection information needed to handle a request, such as an IP address. Course names and course identifiers can reveal the area you are interested in or playing. We do not claim that these providers discard requests or retain no logs. Their own practices apply to information they receive.

Links to official golf rules and other external destinations open outside PURE ARC's local content. The destination service handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Monthly and lifetime Apple purchases

Apple handles checkout, billing, and payment credentials for monthly Full Access subscriptions and one-time lifetime Full Access purchases. PURE ARC does not receive your credit-card number. Through StoreKit, the app checks Apple-verified purchase information, including product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, and renewal, revocation, or refund status, to enable paid features and restore access. The current app does not send these transactions to a PURE ARC purchase backend.

The app uses verified Apple purchase information to enable paid features. Subscription management and purchase restoration use Apple services. Monthly subscriptions renew until canceled; a lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase. Buying lifetime access does not itself cancel a separate monthly subscription. Manage any subscription through your Apple Account. Developer simulations and Apple sandbox/test transactions are separate from real App Store purchases and do not themselves create a paid subscription.

Apple handles information associated with its App Store and related services under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing and exports

You decide when to export a video or golf-data backup, save a video to Photos, or share content using another app. Shared media contains the content and edits included in that export. A shared backup contains the golf records and notes described above. Any recipient, social network, messaging app, or storage provider you choose then handles the copy under its own practices. Deleting something in PURE ARC does not delete copies already saved or shared elsewhere.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not use your recordings to train external AI models.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device while it is needed for your saved Library, rounds, edits, and preferences. Use the available delete controls to remove saved shots, rounds, or Rules history. Removing an audio placement removes it from that edit. Removing a sound from "Your audio" removes it from the reusable list; its underlying local file can remain so saved edits continue to work. Videos shared by more than one shot or Range record can likewise remain while another record uses them. Local caches or recovery files may also remain. Personal-learning examples and profiles can be reset through the available personal-learning controls. Backup recovery versions may preserve earlier golf records as described above.

Deleting the app through iOS removes its app-managed local storage. Offloading the app is different and may retain its documents. Copies in Photos, Files, backups, or other apps must be managed through those services. Keep any recordings you want to retain before deleting the app.

We delete support emails and their attachments, and developer-downloaded beta reports or feedback, within 90 days after the related request or issue is resolved, unless retention is required by law. This applies to copies we hold; information held by Apple or another provider, including provider backups, is subject to that provider's retention rules.

You can contact purearc@proton.me about information you provided to us. We cannot remotely access or erase a Library that exists only on your device or browse your private iCloud backups through a PURE ARC support account. We can explain the available local and iCloud controls. Requests concerning records held by Apple or another provider may also need to be directed to that provider. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Security and changes

PURE ARC uses iOS app storage and permission controls, and its configured network services use HTTPS. Protect access to your device and backups. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

We will update this policy if the app's information-handling practices change. The published version will identify its effective date. Additional cloud, AI, advertising or other information-sharing features will be described as needed before they are introduced, together with any required choices or permissions.
